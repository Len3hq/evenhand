# Comments on gallery projects, moderated by the event's organisers

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-29 · Owner: A

## Context

T3 asks for comments on gallery projects, and for "anti-abuse that means something: rate limits, duplicate detection, and an audit trail an organizer can read". A comment box is the easiest place in a portal to post spam or abuse, so comments must be moderated from the start, and moderation itself must be accountable.

## Decision

- **Who may post:** anyone logged in, on a project that is in the public gallery. Drafts, disqualified entries and replaced duplicate copies have no comments (404, as the gallery answers for them). Comments are plain text of 1 to 2,000 characters, checked by the API and by a database CHECK.
- **Who is shown:** authors appear by display name only; their email never leaves the API with a comment.
- **Moderation:** the event's organisers and admins hide a comment with a reason (3 to 300 characters) and can restore it. Nothing is deleted, so a mistaken hide can be undone and the record stays complete. Visitors and the author never receive a hidden comment; organisers see it marked, with the reason.
- **Deny first:** someone who organises nothing is refused before the comment is looked up; an organiser of another event after it (the same pattern as rankings).
- **Abuse:** a comment rate limit of its own (`RATE_LIMIT_COMMENT_PER_MIN`, 10 a minute per person since [rate-limit keys](20260929-1500-a-rate-limit-keys.md), audited like every 429), and every post, hide and restore is an audit sentence ("Demo Organizer hid a comment on "Glass Signal": Off topic"). The audit row keeps an 80-character excerpt, enough to recognise a comment without copying it all.
- A hide and a restore are conditional updates, so two organisers acting at once cannot both succeed; the second is told the comment is already hidden or not hidden.

## Consequences (including what we gave up)

- There is no editing or deleting by the author: a comment is a public statement, and editing would need a history of its own.
- There are no replies or reactions, and no automatic filtering of content; moderation is by people, with a record.
- Comments are not part of the event export (the fixtures.json shape has no field for them).
